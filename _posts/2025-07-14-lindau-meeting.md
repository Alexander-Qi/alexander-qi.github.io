I was honored to be selected as one of 30 Chinese PhD students to attend the **74th Lindau Nobel Laureate Meeting** (Chemistry), an international forum that brings Nobel Laureates together with early-career researchers.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/post_img/lindau/certificate-given.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

**Highlights of the Experience:**

- **German University Tour**: Prior to the meeting, I joined the Chinese delegation on visits to the University of Bonn, the University of Giessen, the University of Bayreuth, and the Technical University of Munich, where I toured the research facilities and learned how laboratories there are organized and run.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/post_img/lindau/visitinggiessen.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

- **Scientific Exchange**: During the meeting, I met and spoke with several Nobel Laureates. One memorable moment was joining **Professor Ben Feringa** for an on-stage "arm-wrestling" demonstration that vividly illustrated how molecular machines convert light into mechanical motion.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/post_img/lindau/withferinga.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

The week gave me a broader perspective on the field and strengthened my commitment to rigorous research in physical organic chemistry.
