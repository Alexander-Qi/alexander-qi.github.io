The **5th Beijing–Tianjin Graduate Student Organic Chemistry Symposium** was held at Nankai University, bringing together approximately 300 students from Tsinghua University, Peking University, the Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICCAS), and Nankai University.

**Key Achievements:**

- **Oral Presentation**: I gave a talk on my recent progress in studying transient radical intermediates, highlighting how direct kinetic measurements clarify complex reaction mechanisms.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/post_img/nankai/report.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

- **Recognition**: Based on votes from faculty attendees across the participating institutions, I received the **Outstanding Presentation Award**.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/post_img/nankai/awards-crop.jpeg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

This symposium provided an excellent venue for academic exchange and a broad view of current research across the Beijing–Tianjin organic chemistry community.
